How it started

No big launch, no master plan. Just a team that kept choosing to build the next thing together.

  1. 01 Where it began

    A group project we took too seriously.

    Pentech started the way a lot of good things do: an assignment that asked for a working prototype and got a finished product. Five people with different strengths found out we worked better together than apart.

  2. 02 The first real build

    Building for people we knew.

    TUT Guide came next, a campus guide for students who were as lost as we once were. Real users, real feedback and real bugs taught us more than any brief could.

  3. 03 Giving it a name

    From side project to studio.

    Once people started asking who had built it, we needed an answer. We chose Pentech Devs, and started treating the work like a studio rather than a hobby.

  4. 04 2025

    Four products in one year.

    A sports performance app, Digifikile, TUT Guide and DocSecure: fitness tracking, a gamified experience, a campus guide and a certification platform. Different problems, same team.

  5. 05 Today

    Still building, step by step.

    We take on work for founders, students and organisations who want one team that can design, build and launch without handing the job from one supplier to the next.

What we believe (04)

A few things we agreed on early, and still hold each other to.

  • 01 Build together Every project has the whole team behind it, from the first sketch to the last deploy.
  • 02 Start with the user We test with real people early, before we fall in love with our own ideas.
  • 03 Ship, then improve Small, working releases beat perfect plans that never launch.
  • 04 Own the outcome We stay on after launch, because that's when the real feedback arrives.
