How payment works

Simple steps, agreed up front, so you always know what's next and what it costs.

  1. 01 Free consultation Tell us what you need. We ask questions, suggest options and give an honest view of what's realistic.
  2. 02 Fixed quote You get a written scope, timeline and price. The price you agree to is the price you pay.
  3. 03 50% to start A deposit books your slot and we begin design, with regular check-ins along the way.
  4. 04 50% at launch The balance is due when you've approved the finished work and it's live.

Questions

Are these prices fixed?

They're starting points. After a free consultation we send a fixed quote for your project, and that's what you pay unless you ask for changes to the scope.

What if my budget is smaller?

Tell us your budget up front. We'll suggest what can realistically be built for it, or how to launch a smaller first version and grow it later.

Who owns the code and designs?

You do. Once the final payment is made, we hand over the code, the design files and access to every account.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of free bug fixes. After that, you can join a monthly care plan or get in touch whenever you need us.

Do you work with students and startups?

Yes. Some of our favourite projects started as one person's idea. We're happy to help you work out what to build first.
