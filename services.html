How a project runs (05)

No black box. You'll know what we're doing, why, and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We learn your goals, your users and your limits, then agree on what success looks like.

  2. 02

    Design

    Wireframes and prototypes you can click through and comment on before we build.

  3. 03

    Build

    Short cycles with a working version to try at the end of each one.

  4. 04

    Test

    Real devices, real data and real people, so launch day holds no surprises.

  5. 05

    Launch & support

    We go live with you, hand over everything, and stay on call afterwards.
